# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and civil. Critique code, designs, and claims as sharply as you
like; do not aim that at the person who wrote them.

Not acceptable here: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized
language, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption of a
discussion.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review, and any other
project space.

## Scope and enforcement

This is a single-maintainer project. The maintainer decides what falls outside the
standard above and may edit, lock, or delete contributions, or block accounts, in
response — proportionate to the situation and without a formal appeals process.

To report a problem, email `tristan@nextcz.com` with `agent-bridge conduct` in the
subject. Reports are handled privately. Expect a best-effort response rather than a
guaranteed timeline.

For a security vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
